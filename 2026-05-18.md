## Rules

- **Age range expressions**: Use `i starten af [decade]erne` for "in the early [decade]s" and `til sidst i [decade]erne` for "in the late [decade]s".
  - *Til sidst i 30erne* → In the late 30s
  - *I starten af 30erne* → In the early 30s

- **Reflexive verbs with `sig`**: Some Danish verbs require the reflexive pronoun `sig` (himself/herself/itself/themselves) to complete their meaning. The reflexive pronoun comes after the verb.
  - *At udvikle sig* → To develop / evolve (oneself)
  - *At gifte sig* → To get married (lit. to marry oneself)

- **Definite form of nouns**: The definite article in Danish is a suffix added to the noun, not a separate word.
  - *Et par* → A couple / *Parret* → The couple
  - *En drøm* → A dream / *Drømmen* → The dream
